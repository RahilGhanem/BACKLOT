THE LONG NIGHT

Original short screenplay written for BACKLOT test fixtures, purpose-built
to trigger the bounded re-plan loop (see backlot/orchestrator/
line_producer.py): six consecutive EXT/NIGHT scenes, each at a distinct
location, produce six consecutive night shoot days -- past the Risk/
Continuity agent's "more than three consecutive night shoot days" bar for
requesting a re-plan. Characters and events are fictional.


1     EXT. ROOFTOP HELIPAD - NIGHT

Wind hammers a corrugated helipad twelve stories up. DAX (30s, wiry,
bleeding from one hand) sprints for the far ledge as a SPOTLIGHT sweeps
the tarmac behind him. Sirens rise from the street below.

                    DAX
          (into a cracked earpiece)
          I'm exposed. Moving to the fire
          escape.

He vaults a low railing and drops out of frame.


2     EXT. FIRE ESCAPE ALLEY - NIGHT

Dax lands hard on a rusted fire escape landing, three stories above a
narrow alley. He half-climbs, half-falls down the switchback stairs,
boots ringing on wet metal. Below, a WATCHMAN swings a flashlight beam
across the alley mouth.

                    WATCHMAN
          Hey! Stop right there!

Dax drops the last eight feet, rolls, and keeps moving.


3     EXT. SUBWAY RAIL YARD - NIGHT

Dax vaults a chain-link fence into a sprawling rail yard -- dozens of
freight cars standing dark and silent under sodium lights. He weaves
between them, checking over his shoulder. Flashlight beams bob between
the cars in the distance; the pursuit hasn't lost him.

He ducks under a coupling and presses flat against a graffiti-scarred
hopper car as a security patrol truck rolls past, tires crunching gravel.
He waits. Counts. Moves again, faster now, cutting diagonally across a
dozen sets of track, boots catching on loose ballast stone. A distant
whistle. Another patrol beam swings wide and nearly catches him -- he
drops, rolls beneath a stationary tanker car, comes up running on the far
side. The yard seems to stretch forever, track after track after track,
each crossing a fresh roll of the dice. At the perimeter fence he scales
it in one motion, drops hard on the other side, and doesn't look back.


4     EXT. RIVERFRONT PIER - NIGHT

Dax staggers out onto a weathered pier, black water slapping the pilings.
A shape idles at the far end -- a runabout boat, engine already turning
over. RUIZ (40s, steady) waves him aboard without a word.


5     EXT. PARKING STRUCTURE ROOF - NIGHT

Later. Dax and Ruiz jog up a final ramp onto the open top level of a
parking structure, the whole city spread out cold and glittering below.

                    RUIZ
          Car's two levels down. We're
          not clear yet.

                    DAX
          Then let's not stop.


6     EXT. ABANDONED RAIL BRIDGE - NIGHT

Dax and Ruiz cross a rusted trestle bridge on foot, girders groaning
underfoot, a single work light flickering somewhere far below. Halfway
across, Dax stops, listens -- no sirens now. Just wind in the ironwork.

                    DAX
          ...I think that's it.


7     INT. SAFEHOUSE KITCHEN - DAY

Morning light through a taped-over window. Dax sits at a scarred table
while Ruiz bandages the cut on his hand. Both are wrecked, relieved,
alive.

                    RUIZ
          Six locations in one night.
          Nobody's going to believe this
          shoot schedule.

                    DAX
          (a tired half-smile)
          Then it's a good thing it's
          already over.
