THE HANDOFF

Original short screenplay written for BACKLOT test fixtures.
Characters and events are fictional.


1     EXT. INDUSTRIAL LOT - NIGHT

Sodium lights hum over a cracked lot ringed by chain-link fence.
A beat-up CARGO VAN idles in the shadow of a loading dock.

Behind the wheel, MARA (30s, restless) checks a cheap wristwatch
against the dashboard clock. She thumbs a WALKIE-TALKIE clipped to
the visor.

                    MARA
          Talk to me.

Static. Nothing. She sets the walkie down, drums the wheel.


2     INT. CARGO VAN - CONTINUOUS

Fast food wrappers, a road atlas, a DUFFEL BAG on the passenger
seat. Mara pops the glovebox -- a FLASHLIGHT rolls out. She clicks
it on, off, on, off. Nervous habit.

The walkie crackles.

                    DESH (V.O.)
          (through static)
          Two minutes. Dock's clear.

                    MARA
          Copy that.

She kills the flashlight and puts the van in gear.


3     EXT. WAREHOUSE DOCK - NIGHT

DESH (40s, all business) slips out a side door carrying a second
DUFFEL BAG, keeping to the wall. A motion-sensor light snaps on
overhead -- he freezes.

A SECURITY GUARD rounds the corner, flashlight sweeping.

                    GUARD
          Anyone back here?

Desh presses flat into a shadow between dumpsters. The guard's
beam passes an inch from his shoulder and moves on. Desh exhales,
keeps moving toward the fence line.


4     EXT. HIGHWAY - NIGHT

The cargo van runs dark and fast along an empty two-lane highway,
no other headlights for miles. Rain starts, light at first.

INT. CARGO VAN - CONTINUOUS

Desh climbs in through the side door, breathing hard, and hauls
both duffel bags into the back. Mara doesn't look over.

                    MARA
          Clean?

                    DESH
          Clean.

She eases off the gas, just under the limit now.


5     INT. LOU'S GARAGE - DAY

A cluttered auto shop, tools on pegboard, a single work light over
a stripped-down sedan. LOU (60s, unhurried) unzips one duffel bag
on the hood, thumbs through the contents, then nods once.

He slides a battered BRIEFCASE across the hood toward Mara and
Desh.

                    LOU
          Count it if you want. I won't
          be offended.

Mara flips the briefcase open -- banded cash inside. She meets
Desh's eyes. A small, tired smile.
